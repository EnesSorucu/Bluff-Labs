FROM THE TABLE:
QUESTIONS.

Does everyone need their own phone?

No. Both games use a single device passed around the table. Just gather your friends in the same place.

How many players can join?

Werewolf Master supports 4–24 players. Mafia Resistance is designed for 5–10 players.

Does Werewolf need a narrator?

The app guides the night, day and voting phases. Nobody has to sit out to become the narrator.

Are all roles free?

The apps offer in-app purchases. Check each app for the current available roles and purchase options.

Where can I find support and legal documents?

hello@bluff-labs.com is our support address. Each app's privacy policy and terms of use are linked below.
